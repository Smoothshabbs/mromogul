Push-in fittings make pneumatic piping quick to install and easy to change, but only when the tube and fitting are matched correctly.

## Push-in fittings are sized by the tube's outside diameter

PU tube is described as **outside diameter × inside diameter** in millimetres, for example **8 × 5**. The fitting is chosen by the **outside diameter** only: an 8 mm push-in fitting takes any 8 mm OD tube.

The fitting's other end screws into the valve, cylinder or FRL. That thread is usually **BSP**: 1/8", 1/4", 3/8" or 1/2", or M5 on very small valves. So a fitting is described by both sizes, for example **PC 8-02 = 8 mm tube × 1/4" thread**.

## Which tube size for which job?

| Tube OD | Common uses |
|---|---|
| **4 mm** | Pilot lines, sensors, small valves and M5 ports |
| **6 mm** | Small cylinders, valve manifolds, short runs |
| **8 mm** | The general-purpose size for most machine cylinders and valves |
| **10–12 mm** | Supply lines to machines, larger cylinders, air tools |
| **14–16 mm** | Short distribution runs and high-flow supply |

If a cylinder is slow or loses force, undersized tube or fittings are a common cause. Long runs need a size larger than short ones.

## Thick or thin wall?

The same outside diameter often comes in two wall thicknesses, for example **8 × 5** and **8 × 6**. The thicker wall (8 × 5) resists kinking and handles tighter bends. The thinner wall (8 × 6) gives slightly more flow. For general machine piping, the thicker wall is the safer choice.

## Fitting push-in connections without leaks

1. **Cut the tube square** with a tube cutter, not side cutters. A slanted or crushed end leaks.
2. **Push the tube in firmly until it stops,** then pull back gently to check it is held.
3. **To release,** push the collar in and hold it while pulling the tube out.
4. **Use thread sealant** on the BSP thread if the fitting has no pre-applied sealant or O-ring.
5. **Don't bend the tube sharply** right at the fitting. Leave a short straight length first.

## Picking the right fitting shape

- **Straight connector (PC):** in line with the port
- **Elbow (PL):** where space is tight; the swivel lets you aim the tube
- **Tee and branch tee:** to split one line into two
- **Union fittings:** to join two tubes, or reduce from one size to another
