After more than 15 years of supplying maintenance and production teams across South India, Accelfast Industrial Enterprises now has an online store: **MRO Mogul**.

It is the same team at 165 Broadway, Chennai, with the same brands and supply relationships. What changes is how quickly your team can get a price, a quotation and an order in place.

## What you can do with a free trade account

- **See your prices** on every size and part number. End users and resellers each see their own price list.
- **Download an official GST quotation instantly.** It is created in our accounting system in your company's name, with HSN codes and the correct GST, ready to attach to a purchase requisition.
- **Accept a quotation online** and add your PO number. The order goes straight to our team.
- **Track orders and invoices,** download invoice PDFs and your **statement of account** for any period.
- **Reorder in one click.** Choose a reminder interval and we will email you when it is time to restock, with a button to place the same order again.

## Brands available from day one

Stanley, Taparia, Techno, Airic, Totem and Black+Decker: hand tools, socket sets and torque wrenches, solenoid valves, FRL units, cylinders, push-in fittings and PU tube, air tools, brass fittings, and taps and drills.

## How to get started

1. [Create your free trade account](/account/#register). Tell us whether you buy as an end user or a reseller.
2. Add the sizes you need to your cart.
3. Click **Download Official PDF quote instantly**, or place the order.

Have a full indent or BOQ? Send it through the [bulk quote form](/contact/) and we will reply with prices, pack sizes and delivery dates.
